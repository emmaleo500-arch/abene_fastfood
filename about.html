Our Story

Where flavour meets tradition

Sarvoraa was born in a small Lagos kitchen in 2019 — from Abena's love for bold, authentic West African cuisine. Every dish is crafted with fresh, locally sourced ingredients and a generous dash of creativity. From our kitchen to your table, we serve more than food: we serve moments.

We work directly with local farmers in Ogun and Lagos State, ensuring every vegetable, pepper, and grain reaches you within 24 hours of harvest. No shortcuts. No compromise.
